## Maturity

So that "decided" is never read as "built", every capability sits at one of:

- **planned** — ticketed, not yet designed
- **designed** — an accepted decision exists, nothing built
- **draft** — built and visible, not complete or verified
- **complete** — finished and verified against its acceptance criteria
- **live** — in the hands of the people it is for

**An accepted decision is `designed`, not `built`.**
